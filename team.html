Christopher R. Dishop | Principal Investigator

Assistant Professor
Department of Psychological Sciences
Auburn University

Dr. Christopher Dishop directs the RocMob at Auburn University, a research group focused on cooperation and organizational behavior in modern work environments. Dr. Dishop received his BA from the University of San Diego and his PhD in Organizational Psychology from Michigan State University. He spent a year working for the Consortium of Universities of the Washington Metropolitan Area and the Army Research Institute, stationed at Fort Belvoir in DC, before heading to Pittsburgh to work as a research fellow of organizational behavior at Carnegie Mellon University. He is currently an Assistant Professor at Auburn University.
